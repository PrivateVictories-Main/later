# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in Later.

- **Issues and ideas are welcome.** Bug reports, rough edges, and suggestions are all useful. Please include your macOS version and which browser you use. Never paste real links or titles from your list; describe the kind of page instead.
- **Code contributions are not accepted for now.** Later is source-available rather than open source: you may use it under either the PolyForm Noncommercial License or the PolyForm Small Business License, at your choice (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)), and the author keeps the right to license it commercially. Pull requests will be closed without review.
- **Security problems** go through [private reporting](SECURITY.md), not public issues.

You are free to read the code, build it, and change it for your own use under the license terms. See the README for how to build.
